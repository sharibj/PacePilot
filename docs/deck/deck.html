The Challenge

Two problems, one runner

PlannerIt plans in a vacuum
Your plan says "easy 8k, Saturday 9am" the same week you have back-to-back meetings and a hackathon running till 6pm. It never asks how your week actually looks.
PacerIt reports, it doesn't coach
A watch says "pace 5:14" and leaves you to figure out the rest. It reacts to numbers, not to what those numbers mean for you right now.
A coach knows your week and reads your effort. Software should too.
