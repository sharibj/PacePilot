The Challenge

A dashboard reports. A coach decides.

AI Fitness Dashboard
"Your pace is 5:14."
Speaks because it has new data.
AI Running Coach
"Your pace dropped but your heart rate didn't. You're starting to fatigue. Hold this effort. We're changing today's target."
Speaks because something meaningful happened.
Design rule: never speak just because there's new data.

The Wow Factor

Prediction

Live observation
"I was going to let you run this one. But your heart rate's been above threshold for 90 seconds. We're backing off now."
Adaptation

History
"Your last three tempo runs, you faded around here. You're not fading today. Let's use that."

"This is the moment we've been protecting. One kilometer left. Now you can race."
